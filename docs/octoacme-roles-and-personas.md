# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Receive specifications and acceptance criteria from **Product Managers**
- Report progress and risks to **Project Managers**
- Review and validate technical decisions with **Technical Architect**
- Coordinate with **Quality Assurance Lead** on test requirements and quality standards
- Support **Change Manager** during release and deployment phases

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Provide product vision and requirements to **Developers** and **Technical Architect**
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on delivery timelines and scope
- Engage with **Stakeholder Communications Manager** on messaging and customer feedback
- Work with **Risk Officer** to assess market and product risks
- Partner with **Change Manager** on adoption strategy and user training

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Track **Developer** progress and manage capacity with **Resource Manager**
- Align with **Product Managers** on scope and priorities
- Escalate risks to **Risk Officer** and **Change Manager**
- Coordinate timeline and communication with **Stakeholder Communications Manager**
- Work with **Technical Architect** on technical feasibility and dependencies

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead owns quality standards, testing coordination, and defect management across all project phases. They ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria and minimize production issues.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards and testing strategies
- Coordinate testing activities across development phases
- Identify, track, and prioritize defects and quality issues
- Review acceptance criteria with **Product Managers** and **Developers**
- Provide quality metrics and testing reports to **Project Managers**
- Recommend improvements to processes and tools

### Authority & Decision-Making Scope
- Authority to block releases if critical quality issues exist
- Define test coverage requirements and standards
- Approve testing approaches and quality gates

### Goals
- Ensure delivered software meets quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects reaching production environments
- Improve testing efficiency and coverage over time
- Maintain visibility into project quality risks

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup participation and sprint reviews
- Quality metrics reports to **Project Managers** and **Product Managers**
- Defect triage meetings with **Developers**
- Quality planning and retrospective sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Developers** on test requirements, automation, and defect resolution
- Align with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- Report quality status and risks to **Project Managers**
- Support **Technical Architect** in testing complex technical solutions
- Coordinate with **Change Manager** on testing and validation for releases
- Work with **Risk Officer** to assess quality-related risks

---

## Stakeholder Communications Manager

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Communications Manager is responsible for managing stakeholder engagement, expectations, and communication flows. They ensure alignment, transparency, and timely information sharing across all project stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain stakeholder communication plans
- Prepare and distribute project updates, status reports, and announcements
- Manage stakeholder feedback and concerns
- Coordinate communication during critical milestones and releases
- Ensure consistent messaging across all communication channels
- Identify and escalate communication gaps or misalignments

### Authority & Decision-Making Scope
- Authority to define communication frequency and channels
- Determine message content and timing for stakeholder updates
- Escalate communication issues to **Project Managers** or leadership

### Goals
- Maintain alignment and transparency with all stakeholders
- Reduce communication gaps and misunderstandings
- Build trust and confidence in project progress
- Enable informed decision-making through timely information

### Typical Communication
- Weekly stakeholder updates and newsletters
- Milestone announcements and release communications
- Escalation reports on communication issues
- Feedback compilation and analysis

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** for project status and risk updates
- Partner with **Product Managers** on feature announcements and value messaging
- Work with **Change Manager** on adoption and readiness communications
- Support **Risk Officer** in communicating risk mitigation strategies
- Ensure **Developers** and **Quality Assurance Lead** feedback is reflected in updates
- Coordinate with **Technical Architect** on technical aspects of communication

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Architect provides technical guidance and makes architecture decisions during planning and execution phases. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Review and validate technical approach and architecture decisions
- Provide technical guidance on design and implementation
- Assess technical feasibility of requirements
- Identify technical dependencies and risks
- Mentor **Developers** on architectural principles and patterns
- Ensure consistency with technical standards and best practices

### Authority & Decision-Making Scope
- Authority to approve or recommend changes to technical architecture
- Veto power over solutions that conflict with technical strategy
- Define technical standards and guidelines

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and performant
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable knowledge sharing and skill development within the team
- Align short-term decisions with long-term technical strategy

### Typical Communication
- Technical design review meetings
- Architecture documentation and decision records
- Code review guidance and technical mentoring
- Technical risk assessments

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Developers** on design reviews and implementation guidance
- Review requirements with **Product Managers** for technical feasibility
- Advise **Project Managers** on technical risks and timeline implications
- Partner with **Quality Assurance Lead** on testability and quality approaches
- Support **Risk Officer** in assessing technical risks
- Work with **Change Manager** on technical rollout and deployment strategies

---

## Risk Officer

### Role Summary
The Risk Officer is responsible for identifying, assessing, and mitigating project risks across all domains—technical, schedule, resource, quality, and organizational. They enable proactive risk management and informed decision-making.

### Responsibilities
- Identify potential risks across technical, schedule, resource, quality, and organizational areas
- Assess risk probability and impact
- Develop and track risk mitigation strategies
- Maintain risk register and escalation protocols
- Provide risk assessments to support decision-making
- Facilitate risk workshops and retrospectives

### Authority & Decision-Making Scope
- Authority to escalate high-impact risks to leadership
- Recommend project adjustments based on risk analysis
- Approve or challenge mitigation strategies

### Goals
- Minimize unexpected issues and project disruptions
- Enable informed trade-off decisions
- Build organizational resilience through proactive risk management
- Establish risk-aware culture and processes

### Typical Communication
- Risk register reviews and updates
- Risk escalation reports
- Risk assessment workshops
- Stakeholder briefings on key risks

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Gather risk input from **Developers**, **Technical Architect**, and **Quality Assurance Lead**
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on risk tracking and escalation
- Review risks with **Product Managers** on scope and priority changes
- Work with **Stakeholder Communications Manager** on risk communication
- Partner with **Change Manager** on organizational change risks
- Support **Resource Manager** in assessing resource-related risks

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
The Change Manager is responsible for managing organizational change impacts and ensuring successful adoption during release and deployment phases. They facilitate smooth transitions and minimize resistance to change.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change management and adoption strategies
- Identify stakeholders affected by changes
- Create training and communication materials
- Coordinate release readiness and deployment activities
- Manage organizational resistance and address concerns
- Measure adoption and success metrics
- Support post-release stabilization and optimization

### Authority & Decision-Making Scope
- Authority to recommend delaying releases if adoption readiness is insufficient
- Approve training and communication materials
- Escalate adoption risks and readiness issues

### Goals
- Ensure smooth adoption and minimal disruption during releases
- Achieve targeted adoption rates and user satisfaction
- Reduce post-release issues and support costs
- Build organizational capability for future changes

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and readiness reports
- Training session facilitation
- Adoption metrics and feedback summaries
- Release communications and user guides

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on deployment timeline and rollout strategy
- Work with **Product Managers** on feature adoption and user training
- Partner with **Stakeholder Communications Manager** on release announcements and messaging
- Collaborate with **Developers** and **Quality Assurance Lead** on deployment validation
- Support **Technical Architect** on technical rollout and cutover planning
- Coordinate with **Risk Officer** on adoption and change-related risks

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
The Resource Manager is responsible for optimizing resource allocation and capacity planning. They ensure the team has the right skills and availability to deliver project commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Assess resource requirements and skill needs
- Allocate team members to projects and tasks
- Monitor resource capacity and utilization
- Identify and mitigate resource constraints and conflicts
- Plan for skill development and cross-training
- Report on resource status and forecast resource needs

### Authority & Decision-Making Scope
- Authority to allocate team members across projects
- Approve or recommend adjustments to project timelines based on resource availability
- Make decisions on skill development and training investments

### Goals
- Optimize resource utilization and reduce idle time
- Ensure adequate skills and capacity for project delivery
- Reduce schedule delays due to resource constraints
- Build organizational capability through strategic skill development

### Typical Communication
- Resource capacity and allocation reports
- Resource constraint escalations
- Skill gap analysis and training plans
- Forecast reports for future resource needs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on resource needs and capacity planning
- Monitor **Developer** and team capacity and utilization
- Work with **Risk Officer** on resource-related risks
- Support **Stakeholder Communications Manager** on resource updates to stakeholders
- Collaborate with **Change Manager** on resource needs for training and adoption activities
- Partner with **Technical Architect** on skill requirements for technical initiatives
